Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common Crave questions.

Account and login

Choose Register from the Crave homepage and complete your name, mobile number, Gmail address and password. Accept the terms to pass the frontend checks. Account creation will work once a secure authentication backend is connected.
Open the Login page, enter your registered Gmail address and password, then select Log in. The current project is a frontend demo and does not yet verify credentials.
Choose Forgot password on the Login page and enter the Gmail address associated with your account. A real reset email requires a secure authentication backend.
Crave currently checks for a Gmail address ending in @gmail.com. Check for spelling mistakes, spaces or a missing domain.
Mobile-number changes should be handled through a verified account-settings flow. That feature requires a connected backend; contact support for guidance in this demo.

Food ordering

Browse the Food vendors section, choose a vendor, open its menu and select Add + on a dish. Open your cart to review the items and enter your campus delivery details.
Open a vendor menu and press Add + on the dish you want. You can increase or decrease quantities in the cart. The demo cart supports items from one vendor at a time.
The current Crave project does not include order history. The Place demo order action displays a confirmation but does not save an order to an account.
Check that your cart contains items and that Block name, Floor / room and your 8-digit registration ID are completed. This project places demo orders only; it does not connect to a restaurant or payment service.

Payment

No real payment methods are connected in this project. The checkout is a demonstration and does not collect payment information.
Crave does not currently process payments. If you were redirected by another service, contact that provider directly and do not share passwords or one-time codes.
No payment is taken by this demo, so it cannot issue refunds. For a real transaction made elsewhere, contact the merchant or payment provider.

Order management

The current demo does not send orders to vendors, so it has no live cancellation flow. A production version should provide cancellation before the vendor begins preparing an order.
Live order tracking is not part of the current frontend demo. It would require a backend integration with vendor order updates.

NEED A PERSON?

Talk to our support team.

Tell us what’s going on and include enough detail for us to understand the issue. This demo shows a local confirmation; connect a backend to receive and manage support requests.
